What you are hearing

Exponential sine sweep Farina · room impulse response measurement

x(t)=sin[2πf1L(et/L−1)],L=Tln(f2/f1),finst(t)=f1et/L

f1 = 600 Hz, f2 = 4.4 kHz, T = 0.85 s. Equal time per octave — which is why it bends upward on a linear frequency axis.

FM synthesis Chowning

y(t)=sin(ωct+β(t)sinωmt)=∑n=−∞∞Jn(β)sin((ωc+nωm)t)

fc = 2.9 kHz, fm = 190 Hz, β(t) = 5.5 sin²(πt/T), T = 0.95 s. Sidebands every fm; the dark gaps inside the diamond are zeros of the Bessel functions Jn(β).

Fourier series band-limited sawtooth

s(t)=∑n=212sin(nφ(t))n,φ(t)=2π∫0tf0(τ)dτ,nf0<4.6 kHz

f0(t) = 380 Hz + 160 Hz·t/T + 14 Hz·sin(2π·5.5 Hz·t)·t/T — a glide with growing vibrato. The fundamental is left out so the stack stays clear of the labels.

sinc impulse response of the ideal low-pass

sinc(x)=sin(πx)πx,finst(t)=1.9 kHz+2.5 kHz·sinc(x(t)),x∈[−6.5,6.5]

A tone whose frequency traces the sinc: main lobe, decaying side lobes, zero crossings at every integer x. The kernel behind every resampler.

The lettering additive synthesis

xtext(t)=∑kmk(t)sin(2πfkt+φk),fk=3k·fsN

mk(t) is row k of the rendered word “LimitlessGreen”. Tones sit on every third FFT bin centre: a Hann window spreads a bin-centred tone over exactly ±1 bin, so neighbours never beat.

Room forest reverb

h(t)=n(t)e−t/70 ms,y=x+g·(x*h)

Exponentially decaying noise as impulse response, applied to everything except the lettering, which stays dry and crisp.

The picture short-time Fourier transform

X[m,k]=∑n=0N−1x[n+mR]w[n]e−j2πkn/N,LdB=20log10|X|

Hann window w, N = 1024, hop R = 168, fs = 24 kHz, 78 dB display range.

The beam phosphor persistence

I(x,t)=v(x)(ρ+Fe−(t−tx)/τ)fort≥tx

ρ = 0.52 afterglow (0.66 in light mode), F = 1.25 flare, τ = 0.55 s; tx is when the beam passes column x.
